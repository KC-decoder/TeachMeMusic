# Product Requirements

Stable, high-level product requirements already established for the
project. Detailed product behavior that has **not** been decided is not
recorded here. Conventions: [README.md](README.md).

## REQ-PROD-001 — Learner-selected musical material

Status:
Accepted

Introduced:
DEV-002

Requirement:
TeachMeMusic shall allow the learner to provide (select) the musical
material they want to learn.

Rationale:
The product experience starts from the learner's chosen piece, not from a
catalog curated by TeachMeMusic.

## REQ-PROD-002 — Initial instrument: piano

Status:
Accepted

Introduced:
DEV-002

Requirement:
TeachMeMusic shall initially support piano as the instrument.

Rationale:
Focusing the initial scope on a single instrument keeps Stages A–D
tractable; broader instrument support is a future consideration.

## REQ-PROD-003 — Structured, interactive learning experience

Status:
Accepted

Introduced:
DEV-002

Requirement:
TeachMeMusic shall transform the selected musical material into a
structured, interactive learning experience designed to teach the learner
how to play it.

Rationale:
This is the core product promise: from "a score" to "learning the piece,"
not merely analysis or display.

Acceptance Criteria:
- TBD across Stages C/D (planning and evaluation) design.

## REQ-PROD-004 — Proficiency level selection

Status:
Accepted

Introduced:
DEV-002

Requirement:
TeachMeMusic shall initially let the learner select a proficiency level
from Beginner, Intermediate, or Advanced, and the same piece shall
support different learning strategies for different levels.

Rationale:
The same piece must result in different learning strategies per level;
level is learner-selected, not inferred.
