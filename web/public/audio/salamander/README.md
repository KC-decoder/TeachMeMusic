# Salamander Grand Piano samples

These 30 MP3 samples are the local piano instrument used by the DEV-005
browser playback adapter. They are a compact subset distributed by the
official Tone.js examples and are pitch-shifted by `Tone.Sampler` to cover the
full MIDI range.

- Instrument: **Salamander Grand Piano V3**
- Creator: **Alexander Holm**
- Licence: **Creative Commons Attribution 3.0 Unported (CC BY 3.0)**
- Upstream sample path: <https://tonejs.github.io/audio/salamander/>
- Licence: <https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/3.0/>

The filenames and SHA-256 digests used by TeachMeMusic are fixed in
`manifest.json`. No sample is loaded from the network at application runtime.
